Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026

1. Order Cancellation

Because food is prepared fresh upon receiving an order, customers can cancel an order only within 5 minutes of placing it by calling us directly at +91 9370828484. Once food preparation has started, cancellations cannot be accepted.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are applicable in the following scenarios:

  • Payment was deducted from your account but the order failed or was rejected by the restaurant.
  • Incomplete or damaged items delivered, subject to immediate verification upon delivery.
3. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds for online payment transactions will be credited back to the original payment source (UPI/Bank Account) within 5 to 7 working days.
